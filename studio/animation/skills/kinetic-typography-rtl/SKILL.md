# Persian & RTL Kinetic Shock Typography Skill

## 1. Core Retention Laws (Anti-Static-Card Rules)
1. **Ban Static Multi-Sentence Cards:** Never leave a rectangular caption box sitting still for 15–20 seconds. Break narration into **rapid 4–6 second kinetic beats** (1–5 words per layer, one dominant idea per beat).
2. **Fill the Frame with Scale Hierarchy:** Giant Hero words/numbers (`125px–220px` on `1920×1080`) carry the emotion; setup kickers (`38px–52px`) and punchline stamps (`36px–46px`) frame them with extreme size contrast (`3:1` to `4:1`).
3. **Nothing Simply Fades In:** Every layer enters with physical momentum (`Slam`, `RTL Whip-Slide`, `Spring Pop`, `Diagonal Slice`, `Glitch Lock`, `Echo Stack`) and exits fast (`Zoom-Through Camera 1.0x → 4.0x`, `Whip-Left`, or `Shatter Dissolve` in `0.16s–0.24s`).
4. **Persian/Arabic RTL Shaping Integrity:**
   - **NEVER split Persian/Arabic words into individual letters** (`letter-spacing` must remain `0`; cursive joins break if split). Animate **whole words, numbers, or masked RTL wipes (`right → left`)**.
   - Entrances travel **Right → Left** (matching RTL eye flow) or **Z-Axis Slam (`2.8x → 1.0x`)** straight onto the focal zone.
